StayLock Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 27, 2026

StayLock is a focus app developed by Aadee Inc. This Privacy Policy explains what information StayLock collects, how it is used, and the choices users have regarding their information.

Information We Collect

Account Information

StayLock uses Aadee Account authentication powered by Clerk. When you create or use an account, we may process information such as:

  • email address
  • name or display name, if provided
  • account identifier

This information is used to authenticate users, maintain accounts, and provide access to StayLock.

Authentication Services

Authentication is provided through Clerk. Clerk may process information necessary to securely sign users in and manage account sessions.

App Usage and Session History

StayLock stores focus-session history, including session duration, completion status, warnings, and jailbreak events, locally on the user's device.

This session history is not currently uploaded to our servers and is not synchronized across devices.

Settings

Preferences such as theme, motion sensitivity, alarm settings, and other StayLock settings may be stored locally on the user's device.

Camera and Augmented Reality

StayLock uses camera access to provide the augmented reality jail placement experience.

Camera imagery is used for the AR experience and is not intentionally recorded, stored, or uploaded by StayLock.

Motion Data

StayLock uses device motion and sensor data to detect when the device is moved during an active focus session.

This motion data is processed for the functionality of the active session and is not intentionally stored as a persistent motion history or sold to third parties.

How We Use Information

We use information to:

  • authenticate users
  • operate and improve StayLock
  • provide focus-session functionality
  • maintain user accounts
  • provide customer support
  • protect the security and integrity of the app

Third-Party Services

StayLock uses third-party service providers that may process limited user information as necessary to provide their services.

These may include:

  • Clerk for authentication and account management
  • Supabase for limited account-related backend services
  • Apple platform services and APIs

These providers process information according to their own privacy policies and applicable agreements.

Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information.

We do not share personal information with third parties for behavioral advertising.

Information may be shared with service providers only as necessary to operate the app, comply with legal obligations, or protect the rights and security of users and the service.

Data Retention

Account information may be retained while the user's account remains active or as reasonably necessary to provide the service.

StayLock session history is stored locally on the user's device and may be removed by deleting app data or using available account/data deletion features.

Account Deletion

Users can delete their Aadee Account from within StayLock through:

Settings → Aadee Inc. Account → Delete Account

Deleting an account removes the user's authentication account and associated account data handled by our services, subject to any legally required retention.

Local StayLock data associated with that account is also removed from the device as part of the account deletion flow.

Children's Privacy

StayLock is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law.

If we learn that personal information was collected improperly, we will take appropriate steps to remove it.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures intended to protect user information. However, no electronic system or method of transmission is completely secure.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When changes are made, the updated policy will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
